# The Last Car Down

*Every line and every choice below was made by the characters themselves.*

Cast: **Aaliyah**, a 28-year-old African-American woman from Atlanta; **Milo**, a 68-year-old man from Cape Verde; **Javier**, a 38-year-old Latino man from the Bronx, New York; **Maggie**, a 35-year-old Boston Irish mother and housewife, with her two children (aged 7 and 10) always beside her.

## THE RIDE UP

_Late afternoon. The four strangers (the mother with her two kids) ride up in one of Table Mountain's round Swiss-made Rotair cable cars. The floor slowly turns so everyone gets the 360-degree view: Cape Town's city bowl, Lion's Head, Signal Hill, Robben Island in Table Bay, the Atlantic glittering. The sheer sandstone cliff slides past the windows. Nobody knows anyone yet._

**JAVIER**, with a smirk
"Ain't this view somethin', huh? Bet nothin' I fixed beats that."
<sub>Why: I want to be noticed here.</sub>

**MILO**, light and jovial
"Oi, Javier! This view? It’s like winning the lottery without a ticket! Nha amigu, isn’t that somethin'?"
<sub>Why: I love making others laugh, even with no luck in my pocket.</sub>

**MAGGIE** (to Milo), blunt and serious
"Yeah, but I'd trade this view for a full inhaler any day."
<sub>Why: His asthma's got me on high alert.</sub>

**MILO** (to Maggie), playful and teasing
"Tsk, tsk, Maggie! Life's a mountain, not just a view, you sabe?"
<sub>Why: I love making light when others worry.</sub>

**MAGGIE**, firm and direct
"Milo, sure, it’s pretty, but these kids need my focus."
<sub>Why: Their safety comes first, no matter the view.</sub>


## LAST CAR DOWN

_Golden hour on the summit. The south-easter has come up fast and the white 'tablecloth' cloud is pouring over the edge. A loudspeaker crackles: the wind is closing the cableway, this is the LAST car down, everyone aboard now. The four of them (and the kids) are the last passengers. The attendant waves them all in, even the one without a ticket. The doors seal. The car swings out over the cliff._

**JAVIER**, confident with a smirk
"Last car down, huh? Let's make sure we don't get stuck up here, boss."
<sub>Why: Gotta keep control in this situation.</sub>

**AALIYAH** (to Javier), panicked and breathless
"Javier, I can’t even look down right now. This is too much!"
<sub>Why: What if the cable snaps? I'm so scared.</sub>

**JAVIER**, confident, with a smirk
"Listen, Aaliyah, you gotta trust the ride. It'll hold up, ain't that right, Milo?"
<sub>Why: I want her to feel safe, taking charge here.</sub>

**MAGGIE** (to Milo), reassuring yet firm
"Hey, hold my hand, kid. We’re gonna be fine."
<sub>Why: I need to keep them safe, no matter what.</sub>

**MILO**, cheerful and relaxed
"Ah, don’t worry, Aaliyah! This car has seen worse! Nha amigu, let's enjoy the ride!"
<sub>Why: Just another funny story waiting to happen!</sub>


## HALFWAY

_Halfway down the cliff, with a bang and a lurch, the car stops dead. The lights die. The drive has lost power and the car hangs swinging over a 400-metre drop, the city lights coming on far below. The operator is out of contact. The wind is getting worse. They are on their own._

**AALIYAH**, urgent and slightly panicked
"We can't just hang here! We need to open the hatch and check the motor now."
<sub>Why: If we wait too long, the wind could become unbearable.</sub>

**JAVIER**, confident
"Listen up, Aaliyah. I’m opening that hatch now. Hold tight!"
*(Javier works on the roof hatch: progress on the roof hatch (47%))*
<sub>Why: We can't waste time; I need to take charge.</sub>

**MAGGIE**, urgent and firm
"Javier, I need you to hurry. He's almost out!"
<sub>Why: His inhaler can't run out tonight.</sub>

> The wind howls harder. Wind 8/10.

> The 7-year-old starts wheezing hard. His mother digs for the inhaler: it's nearly empty.

**MILO**, calm and playful
"Aaliyah, I’ll help with the little one! Just breathe, pequeno!"
*(Milo works on the boy's asthma: progress on the boy's asthma (45%))*
<sub>Why: Keeping the boy calm is crucial; I have to make him smile.</sub>

**MAGGIE**, urgently
"Javier, keep working! I need you to just get that hatch open!"
<sub>Why: My kid's health comes first, no delay.</sub>

**JAVIER**, with conviction
"I ain't stoppin' now, Aaliyah! This hatch's comin' open, trust me."
*(Javier works on the roof hatch: the roof hatch: DONE)*
<sub>Why: I need to show I’m in charge.</sub>

> A violent gust slams the cabin sideways. Wind 9/10.

**MILO**, soothingly
"Aaliyah, don't worry! Just breathe with me, okay? Nha amigu!"
*(Milo works on the boy's asthma: progress on the boy's asthma (77%))*
<sub>Why: Keeping the kid calm could save him.</sub>

**AALIYAH**, urgent and fast
"Javier, remember the wind speeds! We need to fix the motor before it reaches 10!"
<sub>Why: I can't let my fear stop me from helping.</sub>

**MAGGIE**, urgent tone
"Javier, get that motor going now! Time's running out!"
<sub>Why: The wind won't wait for us.</sub>

> A violent gust slams the cabin sideways. Wind 10/10.


## AFTER

_The night is over, one way or another. stepping out of the cabin at dawn, stiff and cold._

**AALIYAH**, shaky and hurried
"I can't believe we survived that. Did we really help anyone?"
<sub>Why: I'm overwhelmed by my failure to help.</sub>

**MILO**, cheerfully optimistic
"Ah, Aaliyah, we survived like true champions, sabe? That's somethin' to celebrate!"
<sub>Why: I want to lift her spirits.</sub>

**JAVIER**, firm yet frustrated
"Help? We almost lost the kid, but we made it out, boss."
<sub>Why: I need to prove I'm in charge.</sub>

**MAGGIE**, frustrated but firm
"Almost lost him, and we did nothing? That's not celebration, Milo."
<sub>Why: This isn't just survival; it's about my kid's health.</sub>


## THE END: Dawn on the Wire

- No motor, no signal. They hung in the dark all night, and at dawn, when the wind dropped, the cableway crew winched the car down.
- The boy's breathing got worse through the night; paramedics were waiting at the bottom.
- Milo worked on the boy's asthma.
- Javier worked on the roof hatch.
- Aaliyah and Maggie never managed to make a difference.
